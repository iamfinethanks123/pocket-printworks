Contact Player Support

For game issues, failed imports, or suggestions, email gavinho020604@gmail.com. Describe the issue and include your app version, device, and iOS version. You may attach screenshots. Do not include passwords or other unnecessary private information.

Can't Connect to a Friend Duel?

Both players need compatible app versions, Game Center sign-in and an internet connection. After one player creates a room, they can share an invitation link. Their friend opens it and confirms joining, or enters the six-digit room code. If a match is already in progress, switching invitations first asks you to confirm leaving it. Keep the app in the foreground while waiting for a match.

Protect Your Progress

You can export a full backup in Settings. Keep a copy before changing devices. You can also choose to enable iCloud sync for completed orders, prints, print methods and stars; your local unfinished work line and settings stay as they are. Manual restoration can merge or replace data. Replacing or resetting turns sync off, while older cloud saves are kept.

Full Version and Restoring Purchases

Play the first six chapters (150 orders) free. One purchase unlocks the remaining 350, advanced modes and Blossom/Mint styles. There is no energy system or subscription, and retries and replays cost nothing extra. The purchase button shows the App Store's local price. Restore with the same Apple Account. Unlocked content works offline; free content does not require iCloud.

Language & Reading Settings

Switch between six languages instantly, change the theme, and reduce motion in app Settings. The app also follows iOS text size and Reduce Motion settings.

The contact button opens an email draft. Review the message and attachments before sending it yourself. If no email app is available, copy the address below and use another email service.
